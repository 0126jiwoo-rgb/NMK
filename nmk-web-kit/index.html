History & SignificanceSince 1945

Why itmatters.

The National Museum of Korea tells the country's story in one place, from prehistory to the Korean Empire. Admission to the permanent galleries is free.

1945Opened on Dec 3, after Korea's liberation
438,366Objects in the collection, 2025
6.5MVisitors in 2025
No. 3Most visited museum in the world, 2025

Visitor ranking: The Art Newspaper, 2025 museum attendance survey.

Timeline

  1. 1945

    Opens on Dec 3, taking over the colonial-era museum building.

  2. 2005

    Moves to its current home in Yongsan, Seoul.

  3. 2025

    Welcomes 6.5 million visitors, the third most of any museum worldwide.
